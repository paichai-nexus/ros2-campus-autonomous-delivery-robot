# Research Scope

## Objective

Develop and evaluate a ROS2-based autonomous delivery robot capable of safe low-speed navigation in a steep and pedestrian-dense university campus environment.

## Primary Research Topics

1. Localization robustness
2. Sensor fusion
3. Path planning
4. Dynamic obstacle avoidance
5. Slope-aware navigation
6. Fail-safe control

## Initial Constraint

The first implementation targets restricted-area autonomous delivery experiments rather than full-campus autonomous transportation.

## Future Expansion

- Campus delivery service
- Autonomous cart operation
- Campus circulation mobility
- Smart-campus infrastructure integration
